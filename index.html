Flagship Project

FutureSeat

Smart College Admission Strategy Platform

🎓

During engineering admission counseling, students face scattered cutoff data and no clear strategy. FutureSeat transforms raw historical admission data into structured, interactive insights — helping students identify safe, target, and ambitious colleges based on their rank.

Features include cutoff trend visualization across years, college comparison tools, and a smart strategy planner for counseling rounds. My role: Frontend development — built the entire React (Vite) UI, CSS styling, and chart visualizations.
